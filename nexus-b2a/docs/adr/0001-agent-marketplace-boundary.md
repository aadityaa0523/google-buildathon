# Make Nexus an agent marketplace with human ownership controls

Accepted during the design interview. Nexus will host discovery of competing sellers, offer comparison, and purchasing across services, data, and physical items; industrial MRO is an illustrative market rather than a permanent boundary. This expands the product beyond a supplier gateway and makes marketplace discovery and seller participation core responsibilities.

The trading interface is exclusively for agents, while humans retain ownership and controls. Routine trades use structured offers and direct transactions; conversational negotiation is optional. This prioritizes low-friction agent interaction while preserving human oversight. The first transaction category, commercial guarantees, payment model, and implementation stack remain undecided.
