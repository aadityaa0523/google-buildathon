## Recommended starting point: invoice evidence copilot

Help a small Indian supplier prepare a structured, evidence-linked packet for a delayed B2B payment. A supplier uploads a synthetic invoice, purchase order and delivery proof; Gemini extracts data, identifies mismatches and missing evidence, and drafts a chronology with links to the original documents. A human reviews the packet. Target CA firms or MSME associations as the first distribution channel.

Why: a specific costly workflow, a demonstrable before/after, a prospective business buyer, and a manageable two-day prototype. The opportunity is not a new government filing portal: Samadhaan, ODR and commercial products already exist. Validate whether document preparation is painful enough to pay for, and check current legal requirements with qualified experts. Do not claim automated legal eligibility or guaranteed recovery. See [India opportunities](india-opportunities.md) for evidence and competitors.

**Demo scope:** three synthetic documents, extraction with highlighted evidence, a missing-document checklist, and an editable chronology. Gemini handles document understanding; Google Cloud hosts the prototype. Use manual review and no live filing or automatic outreach. Measure extraction accuracy and time to prepare the packet on a few examples; label any synthetic results.

## Alternatives

1. **Heat action coordinator:** for one construction contractor or worker organization, convert a sourced heat alert and crew roster into a supervisor checklist and track action completion. Differentiation is execution, not forecasting. Distinctive, but buyer access and deployment are uncertain.
2. **Localized farm advisory:** for one crop and district, answer questions using a small set of reviewed extension documents, showing citations and escalating uncertain cases. International precedent exists, but Indian competitors are active and agronomic validation is essential.
3. **Healthcare referral follow-through:** help a clinic track whether a referred patient reached care. Real workflow potential, but harder to validate and demo responsibly without a clinical partner.

These are preliminary judgments, not numerical scores or assertions that the markets are empty. Choose based on the founder's experience and access to actual users.

## Next steps before committing

- Speak with 3–5 potential users in the chosen segment: ask about the last time the problem occurred, existing tools, time spent, and who pays. Seek one person willing to review a prototype. Do not present hypothetical interviews as traction.
- Check the application portal and make a concise pitch: customer, painful workflow, existing alternatives, proposed wedge, route to market, demo, architecture, founder fit and validation plan.
- Obtain permission to use sample documents; use synthetic documents for public demos. Test against ambiguous and missing inputs, and show source evidence and corrections.
- Confirm API availability, costs and deployment permissions for the chosen Google stack. Avoid implying integrations or customer commitments exist before they do.
