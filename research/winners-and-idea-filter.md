## Improved idea screening

1. **Concrete insight:** a specific user and a problem with evidence beyond an intuitive story.
2. **Existing-AI test:** show a real example that a capable general model plus available tools cannot reliably solve without the proposed system. If a prompt alone suffices, reject or radically narrow it.
3. **Competitive test:** name at least two closest alternatives and articulate a demonstrable difference; no unsubstantiated claims of being first or absent from India.
4. **Advantage:** explain a plausible earned moat (distribution, unique consenting data, integrations, trusted network, repeatable process or measurable technical performance), separating evidence from speculation.
5. **Customer and economics:** identify who pays, why now, acquisition route, switching costs and real-world constraints; interview targets before assigning scores.
6. **AI necessity:** specify what Google AI does, what deterministic systems and humans handle, and a baseline against no AI.
7. **48-hour proof:** demo the distinguishing hard part with held-out or realistic data; define failure cases and a measurable metric rather than a slide claiming future capability.
8. **Founder access and legal feasibility:** connect to actual skill and user access; identify permits, privacy and operations gating the product.

Apply filter prospectively to all candidates, including agent marketplace and traveler marketplace. The current B2A marketplace faces direct competition and requires differentiated supply/demand; traveler marketplace faces commercial baggage classification and trust issues. Neither is validated. Earlier generic summaries should not be treated as recommended inventions.
