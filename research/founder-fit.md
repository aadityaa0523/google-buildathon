4. **Safety / evidence workflow:** An additional candidate based on prior projects: turn a user's account and consented evidence into a cited, editable incident chronology and checklist. Existing complaint solutions and privacy risks require research, and the exact paying customer must be identified. Check whether reuse of prior work is allowed by event rules before positioning it as a new product.

## Founder story and open questions

The strongest defensible story is a team with practical experience building AI and accessible, resource-constrained products, with complementary ML and application-engineering skills. Specific role assignments and customer access need confirmation. Ask: which users can the team interview immediately, which problem have you personally encountered, and what will the third founder contribute? Do not claim deployments, customers, partnerships, or expertise not evidenced by the team.

The event evaluates founder profile alongside product potential, market viability, technical architecture and use of Google AI/Cloud: https://startupbuildathon.com/ . Team fit can improve the choice, but founder-market fit requires real insight into and access to the customer.
