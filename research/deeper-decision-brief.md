## First option to investigate: supplier quality

Focus on one visible defect on one component at an accessible factory. A user uploads a photo and batch number; the system proposes a finding for human review and creates a defect and corrective-action record. Gemini could turn the inspector's voice note into a draft grounded in the record, while a separately evaluated visual model assists inspection. Google Cloud can host the workflow. Your CVs show relevant industrial, computer vision, edge and full-stack experience. Indian and overseas competitors exist; differentiation is a proposed narrow supplier workflow, not a new category. See [B2B research](deep-b2b.md) for evidence and sources.

For a two-day demo, use a fixed image setup and a small labelled dataset, retain separate evaluation images, and report measured false positives and negatives. Label synthetic or public data. Do not claim production-ready accuracy, offline capability or customer validation until demonstrated. The decisive next step is speaking to 3–5 quality managers and securing permission to inspect a real workflow. Pivot if there is no accessible buyer, no recurring visible defect, or no reason to switch from the existing process.
