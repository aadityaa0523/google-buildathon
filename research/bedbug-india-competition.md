# India competition check: continuous bedbug monitoring

**Scope / conclusion (checked 14 Nov 2026).** There is already a directly relevant bedbug-monitoring product commercially offered in India: **BugScents Sentry Pro**, from UK Digital Odour Technologies (DOT), distributed nationally by Giridhar Pai Associates (GPA). GPA’s own announcement says it introduced the monitor to the Indian market and that it is available across India through GPA; it is an active product, not merely an adjacent pest-control service. [GPA launch / India availability](https://giridharpaiassociates.com/2026/08/05/expanding-indias-non-toxic-ipm-toolkit-giridhar-pai-associates-partners-with-digital-odour-technologies-dot-uk-to-launch-bugscents-sentry-pro/). DOT’s product page specifies a physical attract-and-capture monitor, for detection and ongoing monitoring, with users checking the device on a schedule (it says leave 72 hours before checking; glueboard/lure replacement every three months). [DOT Sentry Pro product page](https://www.digitalodourtechnologies.com/sentry-pro).

This is the strongest India competitor found for a new bedbug-monitoring offering, and it means the space cannot be described as an India-first bedbug monitor. The product is materially different from continuous IoT reporting: public product materials describe a passive physical trap requiring inspection, not a room-identified sensor alert to a hotel dashboard. A differentiated digital concept could still test whether hotels value unattended per-room alerts and maintenance workflows, but must prove a buyer will pay extra for quicker signal/response and validate efficacy head-to-head. Do not imply BugScents lacks any unpublicized connected features beyond what its public materials describe.

## Named competitors and India access

- **Spotta (UK):** Spotta currently markets 24/7 IoT Bed Pods for hotels, with room-number detection alerts, own connectivity/power, and reports of deployments in live properties including Bankside Hotel (Marriott International). Its official site reports 22,000+ Pods deployed overall. [Spotta official product/customer claims](https://www.spotta.co/). Searches of Spotta’s official site for India-specific distributor, installation, or customer references found none. This is absence of public evidence, **not proof India access is unavailable**; Spotta solicits global hotel customers, so direct export or sales may be possible. [Spotta about/team](https://www.spotta.co/about-us).
- **Valpas (Finland):** Hotel-specific bed legs act as passive interception/prevention and are sold with a hotel certification/service proposition. Finnish hotel customer pages document installation (e.g., Original Sokos Hotel Puijonsarvi). [Sokos Hotels Valpas page](https://www.sokoshotels.fi/en/hotels/kuopio/original-sokos-hotel-puijonsarvi/valpas-technology). Search did not reveal a Valpas India distributor, hotel deployment, or India-specific offer. Treat Indian availability as **unverified**, not absent.
- **Rentokil PCI India / PestConnect:** Official India materials show broad bedbug inspection/treatment/follow-up service and many local branches, but the India pages found do not market continuous bedbug sensor monitoring. Rentokil’s global business has remote monitoring for other pests (not evidence of India bedbug deployment). Thus strong service-channel competitor and possible future bundling rival; not evidence of a direct connected bedbug product in India. [PCI bedbug service](https://www.rentokil-pestcontrolindia.com/plp/pest-control-bed-bugs-treatment), [PCI service network](https://booking.rentokil-pestcontrolindia.com/products/bed-bug-residential).
- **Russell IPM / iBug:** Russell IPM sells non-connected bedbug monitors (e.g., BugRug and Lurking Bed Bug). [Russell product catalog](https://russellipm.com/pest-control/products/), [BugRug page](https://russellipm.com/product/bugrug/). The remote-monitoring device “i-bug” appears in its technical publication as developed with Cimex Store, but no official India distributor/installation evidence was located. [Russell-hosted technical paper](https://russellipm.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IPCNovDec20_p324-327_Remote-monitoring-for-bed-bugs_R-Naylor_et-al-compressed.pdf). India availability remains unverified.
- **TruDetx / Corroventa / HiCare:** Search results did not establish a current India continuous bedbug sensor product. HiCare was acquired by Rentokil PCI; Rentokil PCI lists service/treatment. This is a limited public-web check, not an exhaustive distributor/import registry search.

## Practical implication for a startup pitch

Pitch only a **measurable wedge over available options**, such as room-level, unattended alerts plus hotel housekeeping / IPM response logs, with a pilot against BugScents and ordinary inspection/traps. The product needs reliable sensor evidence, false-alarm control, connectivity/maintenance, privacy-safe guestroom installation, and documented detection-to-action impact. A generic image-recognition or chatbot layer is not the moat. The buildathon can demonstrate sensor-event ingestion, room alert triage, and action audit trail; real-world sensitivity and specificity require controlled field validation.

### Source notes

All competitor evidence above comes from company or distributor pages and a company-hosted technical paper. Claims of availability and deployment are vendor claims. No public India record found for Spotta/Valpas/Russell iBug does not establish lack of access; contact the vendors and Indian hotel/PMP distributors to confirm quote, import, installation, service, and any existing customers.
