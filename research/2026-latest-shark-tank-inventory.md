# Shark Tank India: latest aired season pitch inventory

**Reading guide:** the curated table is a 24-company analytical sample. The later ledger records the primary-source episode coverage; the final provisional appendix fills discovery leads for missing episodes. This is not a full-video review, an exhaustive competitor audit for every company, or a verified financial census.

**Research window:** 13–14 November 2026 (India). **Purpose:** opportunity discovery, not endorsement or a recommendation to clone any pitch.

## Season status and coverage

SonyLIV's official [Shark Tank India series page](https://www.sonyliv.com/shows/shark-tank-india-1700000741) labels it **5 Seasons**. Sony's [official S5 episode catalog](https://www.sonyliv.com/custompage/-80110587) lists 2026 episodes through the Campus Special. Sony Pictures Networks India's [18 July 2026 press release](https://www.sonypicturesnetworks.com/news) says Season 6 registrations have opened; this is a future season announcement, not evidence it has aired. On the available evidence, **Season 5 is the latest aired season**. Independent press reporting says it concluded in March 2026 after 152 pitches ([Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/entertainment/television/shark-tank-india-season-5-finale-investment-report-aman-gupta-namita-thapar-total-deals-10595105/lite/)); this is a secondary season-size estimate, not an official completeness guarantee.

This is a **curated 24-pitch sample**, not a full roster. I could not retrieve a consistent public directory: independent databases differ in counts and category coverage. Pitch names and on-air terms were discovered from [PitchBase Global's independent Season 5 table](https://pitchbaseglobal.in/seasons/5/), which is unaffiliated with Sony; a subset is corroborated by the SonyLIV episode/full-pitch links below. Company pages support current product descriptions, not independent performance or revenue. Payers, monetization and competitor comparisons are analytical inferences where the source does not say so. NR means no reliable figure verified in this focused review, not no traction.

**Deal language:** “On-air offer” means only what the aggregator records as proposed on the broadcast; a founder can decline or negotiate, and diligence can change or kill it. “Closed” is stated only where a source explicitly reports post-show close. A televised deal is not verified funding received.

## What the sample suggests for an India-needed, profitable venture

1. **Start from the payer's costly recurring job.** The strongest non-consumer leads here are coordination/response (Cosmo Health), modular deployment (Uprear Build), finance/admin workflow (Cotopay), and infrastructure/workload efficiency (vryse). A demo is not evidence of a budget; verify who signs and what line item pays.
2. **Software plus execution may be more defensible than a thin app.** Emergency response, prefabricated construction, façade systems and education hardware expose distribution, service-level and compliance burdens. These burdens can be moats only if the company can deliver them profitably.
3. **The AI pitches are crowded by default.** For report interpretation, AI development, generative music, virtual try-on and immersive commerce, demand proof of workflow-level ROI, repeat usage, proprietary data rights, and costs versus incumbents. A generic chatbot or model wrapper is not the insight.
4. **Televised investment is a signal of investor interest, not product-market fit.** Pitch ask, on-air offer, accepted counter, diligence, and closed financing are separate events. The season table often lacks post-show confirmation; treat unknown as unknown.
5. **Do not build an adjacent clone of earlier scans.** Existing research already rejected sanitary-waste service proof, livestock video follow-up, agricultural robot work ledgers, invoice acceptance, construction change-order capture, food traceability, warranty handling and distributor order entry as weakly differentiated or incumbent-adjacent. This inventory should broaden the search, not rename those ideas.

## Evidence limits and next diligence

- The season status is based on Sony's official Season 5 catalog plus Sony's Season 6 registration announcement. Registration does not establish a broadcast; check SonyLIV again for a Season 6 episode catalog before future reuse.
- PitchBase is an independent secondary database and its “deal” field records on-air outcomes. The page itself labels most deal closings as unreported. I did not treat an on-air offer as cash received.
- Most traction and financial claims could not be independently verified from the accessible primary pages within this focused inventory. No revenue numbers are repeated here as fact. If a finalist matters, inspect its actual episode, obtain current company filings/first-party disclosures, then interview a customer and incumbent.
- Competitors are a first-pass baseline, not exhaustively researched or uniformly sourced. Before selecting an opportunity, verify each named substitute's current feature set and India availability, and compare actual price, implementation time and buyer workflow.
- The inventory is intentionally curated and not exhaustive; it over-samples technology, B2B and hardware-enabled concepts while retaining consumer breadth. It should not be read as a census of Season 5.
