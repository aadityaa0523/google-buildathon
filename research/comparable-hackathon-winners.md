# Comparable Google AI competition winners

Research date: 13 November 2026. These are **different competitions**, not verified earlier editions of Startup Buildathon India 2026. The current [official buildathon page](https://startupbuildathon.com/) does not identify a previous edition or its winners; Google's 2025 reference to a GenAI Startup Buildathon has not been linked conclusively to this program. Listings below use organizer sources and describe projects as presented, not independently verified results. Challenge rules differ, and winning a hackathon is not evidence of commercial success.

## 1. Google Cloud Agentic AI Day 2025, India

[Google's official report](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/company-news/seizing-our-moment-in-guinness-world-records/) describes a 30-hour in-person Google Cloud / Hack2skill challenge with six given problem statements and identifies a winner for each. This is the closest format and organizer overlap found, though it is not established as the same program. These are category winners, not a ranked top six.

| Winning team / project | Problem and concept | Distinctive product / engineering feature |
|---|---|---|
| Arealis Agents | City data overload | Combines urban data and real-time analytics for decision-making and resource allocation. |
| GuruMitra | Teachers in multi-grade classrooms | Offline-first, edge-deployed teacher assistant for lesson materials and multilingual learning. Google reports a 70% lesson-preparation reduction; methodology not available in the summary. |
| Blue Bird / CrowdSense | Crowd safety | Sensor-based crowd intelligence and incident dispatch workflow. Claims of autonomous dispatch are prototype descriptions, not validated real-world safety. |
| Kovai Shines | On-demand help for farmers | Voice farming companion with crop-health analysis and market information. |
| Raah / Raseed | Receipt management | OCR and multilingual voice input paired with Google Wallet and a finance interface. |
| unicorn.ai / Artha | Personal finance | Financial guidance and human-in-the-loop browser-based tax-filing workflow. |

Google's reported registrations differ from in-person participants; do not treat 57,000 registrations as 57,000 onsite competitors. Independent institutional reports add details about [GuruMitra](https://lnmiit.ac.in/congratulations-to-team-gurumitra-clinched-1st-place-at-the-google-cloud-agentic-ai-day-hackathon-2025/) and [Arealis](https://www.vit.edu/AIDSC/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/ISSUE-2.1_AIDS-Monthly-Awareness-Bulletin-Spt-2025.pdf), but avoid asserting one overall winner: both institutes report first-place/champion claims while Google's report lists problem-specific winners.

[Google Cloud winners](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/winners-and-highlights-of-the-gemini-live-agent-challenge) describe a competition built around real-time multimodal Gemini Live, ADK and Google Cloud, not specifically Indian startups. Product capabilities below are reported by the organizer; they are not claims of clinical clearance or validated real-world autonomy.

| Award | Project / creator | What it built |
|---|---|---|
| Grand prize | ORION / Aditya Shukla | Voice-directed operating-room assistant for answers and visual information while a surgeon remains sterile. |
| Live Agent category | drone-copilot / Bryen Param | Speech-driven drone navigation and visual inspection with spoken responses. |
| Creative Storyteller | Sankofa / Jeremiah Somoine | Interactive voice storytelling combining family histories, imagery and sound. |
| UI Navigator | Moonwalk / Enaiho Uwas Paul and Aman Kumar Sah | Hands-free, voice-operated desktop navigation and task execution. |

The real-time voice, vision and action loop is more informative than simply noting the names of the tools. ORION would require appropriate clinical validation before real clinical use.

## 4. Gemma 3n Impact Challenge 2025, global

[Google's award announcement](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/developers-changing-lives-with-gemma-3n/) describes projects built around on-device multimodal Gemma. Positions below are explicit award categories; technical and performance figures are organizer/developer descriptions.

| Award | Project | What it built |
|---|---|---|
| First | Gemma Vision | Chest-mounted camera and voice/controller-triggered assistant for people with visual impairments; developer's blind brother informed design. |
| Second | Vite Vere Offline | Offline image-to-spoken-steps assistant for people with cognitive disabilities. |
| Third | 3VA | Personalized conversion of pictograms into richer communication for a person with cerebral palsy. |
| Fourth | Sixth Sense for Security Guards | Motion-triggered contextual video monitoring. Claimed throughput requires independent testing. |
| Unsloth prize | Dream Assistant | Fine-tuned speech assistant for a user with speech impairment. |
| Ollama prize | LENTERA | Offline education service broadcast from a local Wi-Fi microserver. |
| LeRobot prize | Graph-based Cost Learning and Gemma 3n for Sensing | Robot sensing and planning using a model of sensing latency. |
| NVIDIA Jetson prize | My (Jetson) Gemma | Voice interface on a Jetson using a CPU/GPU processing strategy. |

These show specific design constraints such as no internet, hands-free input and individual speech, not just a general chat interface. They were judged in a challenge designed specifically for Gemma 3n and offline/edge usage.

## 5. Google Cloud ADK Hackathon 2025, global

[Google Cloud winner list](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/adk-hackathon-results-winners-and-highlights/) was oriented around multi-agent ADK applications. Examples:

- **Grand prize — SalesShortcut:** agent-based sales development covering lead research and proposal generation.
- **Asia-Pacific regional winner — GreenOps:** agents audit and optimize cloud infrastructure for sustainability.
- **Latin America regional winner — Edu.AI:** assessment, study plans and mock exams for Brazilian learners.
- **EMEA regional winner — Nexora-AI:** personalized educational lessons, quizzes and visuals.

These are different awards and not a single top ranking. Multi-agent architecture may fit this event's specific judging emphasis; it is not inherently a moat or proof of product-market fit.

## 6. IndiaAI Impact Gen-AI Hackathon 2025

[IISc CNI's results](https://cni.iisc.ac.in/hackathons/gen-AI-2025-results/) describe a separate India-focused competition with IBM-IISc support. Track winners:

- **Crop identification — Team SAY:** fine-tuned a geospatial foundation model for sparse crop labels, combining optical and radar features.
- **Energy forecasting — Team VISA:** combined time-of-day baseline, TinyTimeMixer and LightGBM through ensemble techniques.
- **Agentic AI applications — AyushmanAI:** appointment, medical documentation and follow-up workflow.
- **Agentic track second — StartupSarthi CA Agent:** financial administration/compliance assistant for MSMEs.
- **Agentic track third — WanderPal:** travel assistant.

This contest explicitly evaluated technical completeness, practical viability, data quality and documentation; the criteria do not automatically transfer to Startup Buildathon India.

## 7. Other direct product parallels

[Covasant's 2025 National AI Hackathon](https://www.covasant.com/en/newsroom/hyderabads-covasant-puts-indias-brightest-ai-talent-in-the-spotlight-at-inaugural-hackathon) reports a VIT Chennai team won with an agent stress-testing, safety and cost diagnostic system. Three runner-up teams addressed discovery, conversational builders and routing. This is directly relevant to B2A infrastructure, but the event was invite-only, and exact ranking within runners-up is not stated.

[Google's GKE Turns 10 Hackathon](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/winners-and-highlights-from-gke-hackathon) selected the cart-to-kitchen AI shopping/recipe assistant as grand prize; V-Commerce Studio won the Asia-Pacific regional prize for e-commerce chat, virtual try-ons and advertising. This event emphasized GKE and microservices rather than startup validation.

## Takeaways for our screening

- Do not claim all winners are novel or commercial successes. Many sound familiar at one-line resolution, and some were built against assigned prompts.
- Strong inspiration comes from the full interaction and constraints: GuruMitra's offline classroom; Gemma Vision's camera placement and physical controls; drone-copilot's speech-to-hardware loop; 3VA's personalized communication. These mechanisms explain why a standard chat prompt may be insufficient, without proving market defensibility.
- For a startup-specific pitch, ask additionally who buys, how the founders reach users, why the system is better than substitutes, whether data or distribution is available, and what can be measured. The [current event](https://startupbuildathon.com/) explicitly considers founder profile, product, market, architecture and Google AI use.
- The user-favored agent marketplace and traveler marketplace remain active candidates; these precedents neither prove nor invalidate them.
