# Biomass fuel acceptance and blend optimization: competitor check

**Checked:** 13 November 2026  
**Question:** Can an independent midmarket product join lot-level quality, boiler behavior and supplier procurement data to optimize delivered useful-heat cost across biomass sources/blends?

## Decision: reject as a fresh software startup wedge; retain only as customer-specific discovery

**The underlying job is valuable and has an existing Indian paying ecosystem. The proposed product is substantially occupied.** BiofuelCircle offers industrial biomass procurement from RFQ through delivery/settlement, supplier performance, price intelligence and assured quality. EAI's BioMetric already sells biomass supply-chain benchmarking and optimal sourcing/price strategy to Indian industrial users consuming at least 25 tonnes/day. Thermax/TOESL combines its own procurement and quality controls, supplier networks, guaranteed fuel, and boiler operation; Thermax EDGE Live optimizes boiler fuel consumption from operational data. Internationally, Valmet sells fuel-diet/corrosion and combustion optimization, APOS measures moisture/ash/GCV/PVC, and industrial control vendors can optimize multifuel boilers.

The reviewed public descriptions do **not** document one generic, vendor-neutral, small-boiler software module that jointly ingests lot-level moisture/ash/GCV/chlorine, actual steam performance and supplier quotes, then recommends an acceptance decision and blend at useful-heat cost. This is a narrow possible feature gap, not proof of a viable or unserved business. The exact features are being offered via marketplace, consulting and boiler-vendor service approaches. A startup should not claim uniqueness or savings until it compares against these options on one buyer's historical lot and boiler data.

## What a defensible residual product would need to do

An independent product can only be tested as a **procurement and acceptance decision-support layer** for a specific boiler/site, with operator/engineer sign-off:

1. Attach delivery/lot, supplier, source, contract and weighbridge values to sample IDs and lab certificates.
2. Normalize delivered price, freight, moisture, ash, GCV/NCV and agreed quality deductions into cost per delivered GJ. Preserve dry-basis/as-received distinctions and lab method, uncertainty and sampling chain.
3. Join each lot and blend to boiler run windows: fuel feed, steam tonnage/pressure/load, boiler efficiency proxy, flue gas O₂/temperature/CO, auxiliary power, unburnt carbon/ash, clinker/cleaning, downtime and emissions.
4. Estimate plant-specific cost per useful GJ or tonne of steam and expose the uncertainty. A cheaper tonne can be worse once moisture, ash disposal, mill/handling load, efficiency, slagging/corrosion and uptime are included.
5. Recommend a qualified trial/acceptance/risk hold or shortlist only among engineer-approved blends. Never write setpoints or control the boiler. Thresholds require the boiler OEM/process engineer and site history.

Moat would need a growing, permissioned, cross-supplier dataset of verified lab properties matched to real boiler performance and operating regimes; a buyer-specific integration alone is not a moat. BiofuelCircle already has marketplace, delivery, supplier and quality data; Thermax has equipment/process expertise and is vertically integrated; EAI has national biomass procurement benchmark data. To win, the independent service must be neutral across vendors and cheaper/quicker to deploy, while earning access to data currently held by suppliers, lab, boiler operator, marketplace and utility vendor. That is a high bar.

## Is there an under-served Indian midmarket niche?

Most plausible discovery target: a single-site paper mill, textile/dyeing unit, rice mill, food processor, distillery or small industrial utility with 1–3 captive boilers, several biomass suppliers/seasonal fuels, and a dedicated procurement/boiler operator. Buyer roles are the plant head/energy manager, procurement lead and finance controller; safety/boiler engineer is veto holder. An OEM/EPC/O&M partner or fuel lab may be the better channel and data source than direct SaaS selling.

But public evidence does not establish an empty segment. EAI's 25 T/day qualification directly covers larger repeat buyers; Thermax shows an 18 TPH site using about 60 T/day and a 4.5 TPH digital deployment according to the parent. BiofuelCircle serves process boilers and small-to-large purchasers. Thus “midmarket” by boiler capacity is not enough. The exact residual segment must be defined by (a) using biomass routinely, (b) >3 qualified suppliers, (c) batch quality variation affecting their specific boiler and process, (d) no existing Thermx/Valmet/EAI/BiofuelCircle solution meeting the need, and (e) authority to share measured plant data.

Economics sanity check (illustrative only): at 25 T/day and assumed delivered biomass ₹5–8/kg, fuel spend is ~₹4.6–7.3 crore/year (25,000 kg × 365 × price). A verified 1% improvement is ₹4.6–7.3 lakh/year before operating costs. This could support low-single-lakh annual software/measurement fees, but only if true savings beat variance and are sustained. A 5% savings claim is unsupported; actual heat-cost delta must be measured on the customer's own lots and boiler. At materially lower consumption, lab/sample effort and deployment can overwhelm the value.

## Adoption and product risks

- Representative sampling is difficult: a truck's stratified/wet fuel may not match a small sample certificate; weighbridge, moisture test and laboratory values can disagree.
- Different biomass types have different proximate/ultimate/ash chemistry; GCV alone misses alkali/chlorine/silica and fusion behavior. The correct acceptance boundary depends on feed and boiler design.
- Fuel-data labels depend on method (as-received vs dry basis, GCV vs NCV, sampling and lab accreditation). Incorrect unit conversions directly misprice the fuel.
- Boiler and process performance are confounded by load, operator, temperature, air flow, steam demand, maintenance and material mix. Historical correlations are not proof a fuel batch caused an outcome.
- A mill may have no suitable boiler sensors, or must purchase/maintain APOS-like online analysers. Lab turnaround can lag procurement decisions.
- Existing EPC/O&M contracts and boiler warranties constrain permissible fuels and blend ratios; only OEM/qualified engineers can approve changes. Emissions and process product quality can veto economic blends.
- Marketplace neutrality is a conflict: BiofuelCircle/Buyofuel may benefit from completed transactions, while an independent optimiser may recommend rejecting a supplier or changing contract basis.
- Integration can require DCS historian access, weighbridge, ERP/procurement, lab certificates and boiler/steam ledger. This risks a services business.

## Google prototype scope (only if a real data partner exists)

Demonstrate a retrospective, non-controlling acceptance and cost comparison: import a small batch of fictional or partner-provided supplier quotes, lab tests, fuel deliveries and boiler run data; compute delivered cost per useful heat and flag uncertainty/quality risks; show why cheapest-ton is not cheapest-steam; compare with the operator's accepted ranges; have a boiler expert approve any candidate blend. Use Gemini to extract certificate values and summarize traceable source documents; deterministic code calculates dry/as-received conversions, heat cost and sensitivity. Cloud Run, BigQuery/Storage and a reviewer UI can handle records. Do not present model estimates as metallurgical/combustion guarantees, or operate live valves/feeders.

## Go/no-go gate

**No-go for a generic company pitch today.** Run one discovery/pilot only if an industrial boiler owner or an OEM/O&M partner can provide 6–12 months of actual lot, quality, purchasing, steam, operating and outage data and has authority to compare it against their current BiofuelCircle/EAI/boiler OEM workflow.

Advance only if a retrospective test finds a meaningful, repeatable procurement decision that incumbents do not already provide; a live, engineer-approved trial then demonstrates lower cost per useful steam/heat without violating boiler or process limits; and a budget owner pays for the trial with a credible repeat site/fuel path. If the customer has no batch IDs/lab data, accepts on delivered tonnage, buys fuel/steam bundled from Thermax, or already uses EAI/marketplace/EDGE/Valmet effectively, stop. No software moat is established by making a blend calculator or marketplace dashboard.

## Source caveats

All performance, scale and savings figures above are company-reported, not independently verified. EAI's site describes a consulting engagement and database; BiofuelCircle marketplace pages and customer case are vendor materials; Thermax pages describe current product/service scope but not all implementation detail or pricing. We did not interview an industrial fuel buyer, run competitor trials, inspect DCS data, audit delivered fuel claims, or verify software pricing. This report therefore supports a competitor rejection and a discovery gate, not a proven opportunity.
